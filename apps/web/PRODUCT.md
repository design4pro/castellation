# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Castellation lets visitors explore literary characters, events, and relationships at a selected chapter boundary. This description is established by the running application and existing routes, not by a new product interview.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current catalog supplies real book titles, authors, character descriptions, and chapter positions.
- Visitors can enter through the library, a character, a guide, or an interactive story map.
- Chapter selection limits the information shown. Existing reading boundaries must remain intact.
- Guest progress and account progress are distinct existing workflows.
- The application has English and Polish routes. Existing locale choices and URL parameters must remain functional.
- The existing Astro application runs on Cloudflare Workers. This design task targets the development web Worker only.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly requests a bookshelf or old-bookshop appearance and 50 new visual iterations, each deployed separately. Existing title artwork and the Castellation name remain product assets.

## Evidence on Hand

The running catalog, existing story illustrations, chapter-scoped descriptions, and working navigation are the available evidence. No conversion experiment, customer testimonial, pricing promise, or measured conversion uplift was supplied.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The user's project instructions name WCAG 2.2, responsive design, maintainability, and end-to-end testing. New reading entry points must remain native links, usable with a keyboard and without client JavaScript.

## Open Decisions

The primary commercial conversion event and its measurement were not specified. Opening a book, map, character, or guide is the working design hypothesis for this iteration series; it is not a claim of measured improvement.
