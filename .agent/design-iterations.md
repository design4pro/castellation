Each row is a distinct change deployed to the development Worker. Version IDs are recorded from Wrangler's successful deployment output. The final quality deployment is recorded separately in `final-deployment.json`.

The conversion hypothesis is easier entry into a book, map, character profile, or guide. Conversion uplift has not been measured.
