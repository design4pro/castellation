# Web design direction - owner decisions

- Date: 2026-10-31
- Owner: Product owner, the developer participating in this discovery session.
- Source: Direct answers in the Castellation product discovery conversation.
- Scope: Website style and design. This record does not authorize implementation.

## Stated assessment

The owner considers the website as a whole insufficiently modern and professional, describing its current appearance as generic AI-generated design and reminiscent of the 1990s. This is the owner's assessment, not a reader interview, independent visual audit, or measured usability finding.

## Confirmed choices

1. The immediate brief concerns the website's style and design.
2. The website should evoke a library, preferably an old library.
3. Literary characters and relationship graphs take priority over books and their contents.
4. When asked to distinguish a contemporary interface with an old-library atmosphere from a literal library interior used as the interface, the owner selected the contemporary interface with an old-library atmosphere. The alternatives were supplied by the agent; the owner confirmed the selection.

## Limits and unresolved details

No reader accounts, audience research, visual references, preferred palette, font selection, usability measurements, commercial targets, or implementation schedule were supplied. The owner's assessment does not establish that readers share it. The existing website is a prototype; its implementation and internal design reports establish previous choices and behavior, not demand or visual effectiveness.

## Consequences

The brief should distinguish the newly confirmed direction from the existing bookshop-oriented documentation. Exact visual treatments and evaluation criteria remain proposals until approved. Revisit this direction with the product owner when evaluating design concepts or when reader evidence conflicts with it. Any change to these choices requires owner confirmation and a superseding record.
